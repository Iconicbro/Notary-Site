What ID do I need?

A current, government-issued photo ID such as a driver's license, state ID, or passport. The name must match the document.

What do I need for an online session?

A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera, microphone, and a stable internet connection, plus your ID and your unsigned document.

Can you tell me which form to use?

No. Notaries who are not attorneys cannot give legal advice or choose documents for you. Please contact the receiving agency or an attorney.
